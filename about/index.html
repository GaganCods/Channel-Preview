Why Channel Preview Exists

The Problems We Solve

Every creator has faced these frustrations. We built Channel Preview to eliminate them.

🖼️

Banner Cropping Issues

YouTube crops your 2560×1440 banner differently on every device — TV shows the full image, mobile shows a narrow strip. Creators often upload banners with logos near the edge, only to see them cut off on mobile.

🎬

Thumbnail Readability Issues

A thumbnail that looks great at full size can become impossible to read when shown as a small preview in search results or the sidebar. Channel Preview shows you exactly how your thumbnail appears at every size.

📱

Mobile Preview Challenges

Most creators design on desktop but the majority of YouTube viewers watch on mobile. Without a mobile preview tool, you're designing blind for your largest audience.

🎯

Need for Accurate Creator Tools

Generic image viewers don't replicate YouTube's exact crop zones, safe areas, or rendering rules. Channel Preview uses pixel-accurate simulations built from YouTube's actual specifications.
