About Channel Preview

Everything You Need to Know
Before You Upload

What Is Channel Preview?

Channel Preview is a free browser-based tool that lets YouTube creators preview their channel assets — banners, thumbnails, and profile pictures — before uploading. Unlike uploading directly to YouTube Studio and hoping for the best, Channel Preview gives you an accurate, real-time simulation of how your content will look to viewers.

Everything runs entirely in your browser. Your images are never sent to a server. You get instant results without creating an account.

How to Use Channel Preview

Getting started takes under 60 seconds:

  1. Choose your tool — Banner Preview, Thumbnail Preview, Profile Picture, or Title Preview.
  2. Upload your asset — drag and drop or click to select. Accepts JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP.
  3. Switch device views — toggle between Desktop, Mobile, Tablet, and TV instantly.
  4. Upload with confidence — once it looks right on all screens, you're ready for YouTube.

Why Preview Before Uploading?

YouTube displays your banner art differently on every device. A 2560×1440 banner is shown in full on TVs, cropped to a 2560×423 strip on desktop, and further narrowed to 1546×423 on mobile. A logo or channel name placed near the edge of your design will be completely invisible to mobile visitors.

Channel Preview eliminates this guesswork. The tool shows you exactly what viewers see on each device — safe area overlays included — so you can fix issues before they go live.
