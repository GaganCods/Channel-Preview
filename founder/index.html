Why Channel Preview Exists

The frustrating cycle of uploading, checking, and re-designing.

The Banner Guesswork

I would spend hours designing a beautiful banner, only to upload it and see the most important text cropped out on mobile devices. There was no easy way to test the safe zones.

The Profile Picture Crop

Square designs looked great in Figma, but getting the perfect circle crop on YouTube required endless trial and error to ensure logos weren't cut off.

The Thumbnail Context

Thumbnails looked massive and clear on my monitor, but when placed next to competing videos in the actual YouTube feed, the text was unreadable.

The Breaking Point

Creators lacked a dedicated, private way to test these designs in context. Uploading private videos just to test thumbnails was slowing down the creative process.

Building The Tool I Needed Myself

Instead of complaining, I decided to build a sandbox. A place where creators could test everything before hitting publish.

Banner Preview

Test TV, Desktop, and Mobile safe zones instantly.

Thumbnail Context

See your thumbnail in the actual feed layout next to competitors.

Profile Crop

Perfectly align your logo for the circular avatar format.

To help every creator confidently publish content without worrying about how it will appear on YouTube.

What We Believe

The core values that drive our product decisions.

01

Creator First

We build tools that solve real, tangible problems that creators face daily. If it doesn't save time or improve quality, we don't build it.

02

Privacy Matters

Your upcoming video ideas are confidential. All previews are generated entirely on your device. We never upload or store your images on our servers.

03

Simple Tools Win

Software should be fast, focused, and intuitive. We avoid feature bloat and focus on doing one thing exceptionally well.
